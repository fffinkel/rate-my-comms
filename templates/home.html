Rate my comms

One-click feedback for emails and Slack posts. You add rating links to a message. Readers click one. That click is the vote. They land on a page with an optional comment box, and can close the tab or type a line.

How to use it

  1. Open Make links. Enter the email subject or post title. Pick Yes/No or 1 to 5.
  2. Click Copy. Paste at the end of your Slack message or email. The links arrive as clickable text.
  3. Send. Check Results whenever you like. Use the same title again next time to keep a running tally.

Slack and email

The Copy button puts formatted text on your clipboard. Slack, Gmail, and Outlook all paste it with the links intact. If something strips the formatting, use the plain text version; it has one bare URL per line and most tools will link those.

Writing links by hand

Any visit to a vote URL counts one vote. Spaces in the title are written %20.

{{.Base}}/vote?key=Weekly%20update&value=yes
{{.Base}}/vote?key=Weekly%20update&value=no
{{.Base}}/vote?key=Weekly%20update&value=4

value is yes, no, or a whole number from 1 to 5.

Good to know

  • There is no login. Anyone with the link can vote, and anyone who can reach this site can see results.
  • Each click is a vote, so one reader can vote more than once.
  • Email security scanners sometimes open links before the reader does. Expect a little noise on big email lists.
